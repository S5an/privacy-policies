# Privacy Policy – TheGame

**Developer:** Andrei (PocketRealm)

**Last updated:** November 24, 2026

## 1. Introduction

TheGame is a mobile game developed and published by Andrei (PocketRealm). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

## 2. Data Collection

TheGame does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to play.

The current version of the game does not contain advertising, in-app purchases, or developer-operated analytics services.

I do not operate a server that collects players' gameplay data.

## 3. Local Data Storage

TheGame stores your high score locally on your device to preserve your progress between gameplay sessions.

You can delete locally stored game data through your device's application settings. Android backup and restoration features may also apply, depending on your device settings.

## 4. Third-Party Services

TheGame is developed using the Unity game engine and distributed through Google Play.

Unity diagnostic data collection is disabled for the current Android release.

Google Play may process information in connection with application distribution, security, installation verification, and platform functionality.

This privacy policy is hosted using GitHub Pages, which may process technical information, including IP addresses, for security purposes.

For additional information, please refer to the following privacy policies:

- [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored game data remains on your device until it is cleared.

If you contact me by email, your email address and the information you voluntarily provide will be used to respond to your request.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as necessary to resolve your request or meet applicable legal obligations.

You may contact me to request access to or deletion of personal information you have provided.

## 6. Data Security

I do not maintain an online database of player information for TheGame. I take reasonable measures to protect information provided through support communications.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when new features are introduced or when the game's data practices change.

The latest version will be available on this page.
